Wedding Events · विवाह समारोह

✦
🌼

Haldi

हल्दी
11:00 AM – 2:00 PM

Turmeric, sandalwood and rose water are lovingly applied to the couple amid songs and laughter — a blessing of radiance for the days ahead.

Wear yellow — marigold, mustard, sunshine, any shade of it.

✦
💃

Sangeet

संगीत
7:00 PM – 12:00 AM

An evening of music, dance and dazzle as both families take the stage. Come prepared to dance the night away.

Bring the bling — sequins, shimmer and your best dancing shoes.

Tuesday · 24 November
✦
🪔

Tilak

तिलक
10:00 AM – 12:00 PM

The bride's family honours the groom with the sacred tilak on his forehead — a mark of acceptance, respect and the formal bond between the two houses.

Traditional attire — safas and sarees.

🐎

Baraat & Wedding

From 6:00 PM

The groom arrives in royal Rajputana style with band, baaja and baraat — followed by the varmala, pheras and the sacred vows under the mandap.

Traditional and regal — lehengas, sherwanis, poshaks.

✦
